# Skill Fusion AI — Customer Guide

Welcome to Skill Fusion AI. This guide walks you through everything you need, from first login to your first graded project.

## Quick start: your first session

1. Register with your email and confirm the verification link.
2. Pick the skills you already have (or upload a resume to pre-fill them).
3. Choose a second skill to fuse — the Fusion Engine explains how they complement each other.
4. Pass the quick evidence quiz for each skill so your profile stays honest.
5. Generate three roadmap previews, pick the one that fits your outcome, and make it yours with the Tweak bar.
6. Follow the roadmap week by week and log real work in the Growth Log.
7. Build the capstone project, then submit either a write-up or the whole project kit.
8. Read the AI's grade and feedback, then generate your resume and cover letter from your verified evidence.

## What each feature does

- Fusion Engine: explains how two (or three) skills complement each other and what industries the pair hits.
- Fusion Score: rates how rare and in-demand your chosen combination is, from real job postings — the weighting is shown, so you can see why the number is what it is.
- Roadmaps: three plans to start, then one chosen plan you can safely rewrite.
- Projects: a real-world brief with graded feedback; whole kits (zips) are graded file by file.
- Growth Log: your evidence record — verified credentials stand out from self-reported claims.
- Resume and cover letter: generated from your Growth Log, exportable as PDF.
- Connect: match with peers who need your skills, confirm a skill exchange on both sides, or join the open lounge.
- Your creature: it evolves as your real progress accumulates — it cannot be bought.

## Frequently asked questions

**What counts as a skill?** Anything you can deliberately practice and evidence: Python, ceramic glazing, Blender modelling, cold outreach.

**Where does the Fusion Score come from?** From live job-posting analysis and how often your two skills appear together in demanded combinations. It is computed, not invented, and the signal weightings are visible.

**How long does a roadmap take?** Previews return in seconds; a full chosen roadmap trades a little longer for a week-by-week plan built around your outcome.

**How do I get verified?** Upload a credential link or file in the Growth Log; verified items get a badge. You can also prove a skill instantly by passing its quiz.

**Can I change my skills after choosing?** Yes — re-fuse your pair into a trio anytime; your history and evidence stay attached.

**Is my data private?** The AI runs on our own infrastructure, so your prompts and projects stay inside it. Chat is moderated, and you can report any peer message.

**My project submission is a full folder of files. Can I grade it all at once?** Yes — submit the zip. The platform reads it file by file under safe limits and grades each file, showing this estimate before you commit.

**What if a roadmap suggestion doesn't fit?** Use the Tweak bar; the model rewrites the actual plan, and the change is validated before it is applied.

**How do I pair skills with another person?** Find a peer in the Connect lounge, propose an exchange, and both sides confirm before it starts.

**What does the creature do?** It represents your logged progress. It grows as your evidence grows — nothing else feeds it.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Couldn't load the quiz — is the backend running?" | Refresh the page; if it persists, the AI service is restarting — try again in a minute. |
| A generation step seems stuck | Steps are honest: if the last label is still spinning, the AI is still working. Large roadmaps take up to half a minute. |
| Upload failed ("Couldn't upload that file") | Check the file type (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD and code files are supported) and size, then retry. |
| A peer message is offensive | Use Report on the message; reports are auto-hidden at threshold and reviewed by moderators. |
| Zip grading is still estimating | Large kits take longer; the estimate shows before grading starts, and you can re-grade from the submission card. |

## Contact and feedback

Use Report in the Connect lounge for chat issues. For account, grading, or engine problems, contact the team through the course support channel; include your registered email and what you were doing when it failed, plus a screenshot if you have one.
